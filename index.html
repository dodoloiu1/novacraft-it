How we work

A process you can actually follow

Four stages, each with an explicit outcome. You always know what happens next and what it costs.

  1. Discover

    We map the current state, the constraints and the real problem. Outcome: a written scope and a fixed-price estimate.

  2. Design

    Architecture, interfaces and a delivery plan your team reviews before a line of production code is written.

  3. Deliver

    Short iterations with working software at the end of each one, demonstrated to your people and deployable at any time.

  4. Operate

    We hand over runbooks, monitoring and knowledge — then stay available on a support plan if you want us to.
